## 1. Supervised Learning (Apprentissage Supervisé)

The model learns from labeled data (we have the "answers" or the target variable `y`). This is divided into two main types:

- **A. Regression (Régression):** Used when you want to predict a **continuous number**.
    - _Example:_ Predicting the exact _price_ of a house (e.g., $250,000), predicting the _temperature_ tomorrow, or predicting the exact _amount of debt_ a client will have.
- **B. Classification:** Used when you want to predict a **discrete category or label**.
    - _Example:_ Predicting if an email is _Spam or Not Spam_, if a tumor is _Malignant or Benign_, or in your case, predicting the _Decision_ (Acceptation / Refus / Risque élevé).

## 2. Unsupervised Learning (Apprentissage Non Supervisé)

The model learns from unlabeled data (there is no target variable `y`). It tries to find hidden patterns or groupings.

- _Example:_ **Clustering** (grouping clients into different segments based on their spending habits without knowing the groups beforehand).

## 3. Reinforcement Learning (Apprentissage par Renforcement)

The model learns by interacting with an environment and receiving rewards or penalties (like training an AI to play chess or a robot to walk).
